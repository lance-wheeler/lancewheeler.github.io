Information I collect

The site itself doesn't collect personal information. There are no accounts, contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, analytics tools, or advertising trackers.

If you email me, I receive whatever you choose to include (like your name, email address, and message). I only use it to reply to you, and I don't add you to any list or share it with anyone.

Settings saved in your browser

Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it sticks on your next visit. It stays on your device and is never sent to me. You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

The "Ask About Lance" assistant

The chat assistant gives pre-written answers and runs entirely in your browser. What you type isn't stored, logged, or sent anywhere. Please don't type sensitive personal information into it anyway.
