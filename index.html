Origin Story

FROM HELP DESK
TO PRODUCT OWNER

Four promotions. One company. A story about showing up, owning outcomes, and building something real from the ground up.

I didn't start in product. I started at the support desk at Stellar MLS — diagnosing issues, learning every corner of the platform, and building a reputation for getting things done.

Over 3+ years, I earned four promotions by taking ownership beyond my job title. Every time there was a gap between what the business needed and what was getting built, I stepped into it.

Today I manage a 9-product portfolio serving 80,000+ real estate professionals — owning everything from requirement gathering and QA, to vendor relationships, compliance initiatives, and internal AI enablement.

The title changed. The mindset was always the same: own the value, not the output.

Case Study

GEARUP APP
SHOWCASE

A real product I led during my A-CSPO training — taken from sticky-note workflow to fully interactive prototype, all under serious time pressure.

During my Advanced Certified Scrum Product Owner course, our team was given a 20-minute time limit to map out a product workflow on sticky notes. That was the assignment. Most teams stopped there.

I went further. After class, I took our team's vision and built out a complete interactive prototype — branded UI, multiple screens, working navigation, and a live hosted demo my classmates could click through. The trainer, Richard Cheng, called it out specifically in his feedback: "I loved your product prototype!"

The Product

GearUp is a mobile app for car owners and enthusiasts to compare and shop modifications, with AR visualization, VIN-based fitment, and cross-retailer price comparison.

My Role

Product Owner — defined the vision using Moore's template, built the backlog, prioritized features against business goals, and personally drove the prototype to a working demo.
