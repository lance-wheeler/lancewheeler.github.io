Origin Story

FROM HELP DESK
TO PRODUCT OWNER

Four promotions. One company. A story about showing up, owning outcomes, and building something real from the ground up.

I didn't start in product. I started at the support desk at Stellar MLS — diagnosing issues, learning every corner of the platform, and building a reputation for getting things done.

Over 3+ years, I earned four promotions by taking ownership beyond my job title. Every time there was a gap between what the business needed and what was getting built, I stepped into it.

Today I manage a 9-product portfolio serving 80,000+ real estate professionals — owning everything from requirement gathering and QA, to vendor relationships, compliance initiatives, and internal AI enablement.

The title changed. The mindset was always the same: own the value, not the output.

Case Study

GEARUP APP
SHOWCASE

My A-CSPO class team project — a real product I led from vision to working prototype in 48 hours.

During my Advanced Certified Scrum Product Owner course, my team was tasked with building a product vision and rough prototype in two days. I went further — leading our team to ship a fully interactive working prototype, complete with branded UI, navigation flows, and a live demo.

The Product

GearUp is a mobile app for car owners and enthusiasts to compare and shop car modifications, with AR visualization, VIN-based fitment, and cross-retailer price comparison.

My Role

Product Owner — built the vision, ran the team, defined the backlog, prioritized features, and drove the prototype to completion. The trainer specifically called out the prototype in his feedback.
